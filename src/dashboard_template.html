Melbourne Guest Experience Insights

A Voice of Guest analysis of written guest reviews of Melbourne short stays, built the way a hotel Guest Experience team would read its own feedback. Data: Inside Airbnb, Melbourne, scraped Sep 2026.

5 things to know

  1. Good ratings hide problems. The average rating is 4.78 out of 5, yet about 1 in 17 reviews still mentions a problem.
  2. Stays near Crown get more complaints: 7.0% of reviews vs 5.3% in the rest of Melbourne. That's 31% more, every month.
  3. Arrival is the weak spot near Crown. Complaints about check-in, keys and finding the building are 73% higher there.
  4. Cleanliness matters most. It is common and it spoils the whole stay. Fix it first.
  5. January is the hardest month (summer heat and the Australian Open). Complaints about value, facilities and air conditioning all jump.

Recommendations for a Guest Experience team

    Method: reviews scored with VADER sentiment (tuned for hospitality phrasing), split into sentences and tagged to 12 guest-journey themes by keyword rules; a sentence scoring at or below -0.05 counts as a complaint. A manual check of 60 random complaint sentences found 45 (75%) were genuine complaints; the rest were mostly neutral context or praise the model misread. Listing ratings are Airbnb's lifetime averages. Built by Dhruv Rawat - Python, pandas, scikit-learn, Plotly.
